# Product Vision

## Vision

Bright Steps Centre Success gives every Centre Director a clear, calm, evidence-backed path through the day while giving Area Managers and specialist leaders the visibility to recognise strong practice, support improvement early, and manage organisational risk responsibly.

The product succeeds when quality, compliance, people support, and financial accountability feel like one coherent operating rhythm rather than disconnected spreadsheets, inboxes, audits, and retrospective escalations.

## Problem statement

Centre leaders balance immediate operational work with continuous quality improvement, evidence, staff leadership, compliance follow-through, budgets, and communication. The information required to act is often distributed across systems and ownership can be unclear. Organisational leaders then see risk late, while good practice and improvement effort are easy to miss.

Centre Success will create a shared operational record that answers:

- What needs attention today, why, and by when?
- What is going well and should be recognised?
- What evidence supports the current position?
- Which issues require a corrective action, escalation, coaching response, or QIP improvement?
- What is changing across quarters and across centres?
- Is each user seeing only the information their role and scope permit?

## Intended outcomes

### For Centre Directors

- A prioritised daily success view rather than another undifferentiated task list.
- Early warning of upcoming work, overdue actions, expiries, and budget pressure.
- One place to connect evidence, internal audit findings, actions, coaching, and QIP improvements.
- Positive recognition alongside risk and exception management.
- Less duplicate entry through governed integrations and reusable evidence.

### For Area Managers

- Consistent quarterly audits and proportionate spot checks.
- Comparable results with the context behind score movements.
- Automatic draft actions from failed internal audit items, followed by accountable review.
- A structured coaching rhythm tied to centre goals without turning coaching into hidden surveillance.
- Visibility limited to their assigned centres except for explicit temporary delegation.

### For compliance and executive leadership

- A command view of overdue high-risk items, evidence gaps, recurrence, and trend.
- Clear ownership and escalation paths.
- Reliable audit trails and source provenance.
- Portfolio insight that preserves centre context and does not misrepresent internal scores as regulatory outcomes.

### For finance leadership

- Governed visibility of approved, actual, committed where available, and forecast expenditure.
- Explainable warnings and accountable commentary without replacing the finance system of record.

## Product principles

1. **Success before surveillance.** Help people know what to do and recognise improvement; avoid punitive or opaque monitoring.
2. **Children’s safety, rights, and best interests guide prioritisation.** The product supports accountable practice but does not make statutory determinations.
3. **One action, connected context.** A task should link to its control, source, evidence, finding, audit, QIP item, coaching goal, or budget period as applicable.
4. **Explain every signal.** Scores, warnings, priorities, and AI suggestions show inputs, rules, freshness, exclusions, and next steps.
5. **Configuration over invented law.** External and internal controls are versioned, sourced, reviewed, and effective-dated.
6. **Backend-enforced least privilege.** Organisation and resource scope are checked for every protected operation.
7. **Privacy by purpose.** Collect the minimum data needed; isolate sensitive wellbeing and child-related material.
8. **Human decisions remain human.** Automation prepares, routes, calculates, and reminds. Accountable users approve consequential decisions.
9. **Modular, not fragmented.** Start with a modular monolith and extract boundaries only for demonstrated reasons.
10. **Mobile-first, not mobile-only.** Core Centre Director work must be practical on a phone; investigation and portfolio analysis should also work well on larger screens.

## Product capabilities

The target product includes:

- daily success priorities and check-ins;
- a reviewed compliance control library, schedules, tasks, evidence, attestations, findings, and corrective actions;
- NQS-aligned self-assessment and a living QIP;
- Area Manager quarterly audits, spot checks, scoring, comparisons, and follow-through;
- coaching and mentoring cycles;
- certification and expiry tracking;
- privacy-preserving wellbeing support and aggregate trends;
- Centre Health with positive recognition and explainable risk signals;
- budget approved-versus-actual-versus-forecast accountability;
- Compliance Manager and executive portfolio views;
- permission-aware AI assistance and governed knowledge retrieval;
- notifications, integrations, audit trails, and security controls.

## Boundaries and non-goals

Centre Success is not:

- a replacement for ACECQA, a state or territory regulatory authority, or official assessment and rating;
- legal advice or a complete digital copy of legislation;
- an HR, payroll, roster, accounting, incident-management, learning-management, or document-management system of record unless a later decision explicitly expands scope;
- a clinical wellbeing or emergency-response system;
- an autonomous compliance decision-maker;
- an employee productivity surveillance tool;
- a reason to expose child, staff, wellbeing, or financial information beyond purpose and scope.

## Success measures

Baseline values and targets require Bright Steps confirmation before Milestone 1. Candidate measures are:

- proportion of Centre Directors who complete a useful daily check-in on active days;
- median time from finding creation to accepted corrective-action plan and to verified closure;
- overdue and repeat high-priority action rates;
- evidence completeness and freshness for active controls;
- QIP improvement progress and frequency of meaningful review;
- audit completion and action follow-through rates;
- time Area Managers spend preparing an audit versus coaching follow-up;
- number and distribution of positive recognitions;
- budget variance warnings acknowledged with an owner and response;
- authorisation-denial and inappropriate-access test pass rate;
- AI answer citation, abstention, and human-correction rates;
- user-reported usefulness, psychological safety, and administrative effort.

Measures must not create perverse incentives to close actions without quality evidence or pressure staff to disclose wellbeing information.

## Assumptions requiring validation

- The organisational hierarchy, regions, centre identifiers, and Area Manager assignments.
- Bright Steps’ identity provider and user lifecycle process.
- Applicable jurisdictions and approved regulatory source owners.
- Existing finance, HR, roster, training, notification, and document systems.
- Audit methodology, severity language, scoring weights, and escalation policy.
- Evidence retention, legal hold, export, and deletion requirements.
- Which wellbeing capabilities can be offered safely and voluntarily.
- AI hosting, model, data-processing region, contractual, and retention constraints.

No assumption above becomes a production rule without an approved business decision.
